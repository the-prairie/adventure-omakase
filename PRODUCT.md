# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web for the current friends release. The preserved native blueprint is a separate future surface.

## Users

Friends traveling in Japan independently, joining all or part of one another's plans and keeping shared memories.

## Product Purpose

A private travel fieldbook: discover an idea, make a specific invitation, choose the part that fits, and keep the memory. Participation is optional; an empty calendar does not imply availability.

## Operating Context

The current release is `apps/friends`, a mobile-first browser app with desktop support. Tokyo, Osaka and Okinawa catalogue entries are research leads. Dates and meeting times use Japan time. Shared trip state belongs to the same-origin Worker and D1; selected photos belong to private R2.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the 300 supplied discoveries, source labels, illustrations, independent participation, personal saves, shared finds, travel windows, invitations, memories, and companion tools. Research must distinguish evidence from inference. Never invent opening hours, bookings, route feasibility, people, or production activity. The local example remains explicitly fictional. Preview, production, live-provider and physical-device acceptance are separate gates.

## Brand Commitments

The canonical brief calls for a warm, editorial Japanese travel journal: calm and trustworthy before a decision, with the interface receding during travel. No casino mechanics, neon gaming, or kawaii mascot direction. The current request rejects cheap-looking dropdowns, cards and incomplete visual walkthroughs, and authorizes research and implementation across the friends app.
