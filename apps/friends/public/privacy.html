Privacy

Updated 7 September 2026

The shared trip stores participant names, optional preferences and travel windows, plans, responses, discoveries, memories and photos in Cloudflare D1 and R2. Other members of your trip can see shared content. Device credentials use an HttpOnly cookie; do not share your private recovery link.

When you ask for suggestions, relevant trip context and your request are sent to the configured model provider: Gemini when configured, otherwise Cloudflare Workers AI. When you explicitly submit a translation or memory draft, the supplied text, resized photograph or audio is sent to Gemini. This app does not retain that submitted media; private text helper results expire after seven days. Saving a reviewed memory shares it with your trip.

When you choose Read booking or Join and read my booking, selected booking images and PDFs are sent to Gemini to propose profile details. This app does not store those files. Extracted drafts are private to your member session and follow the seven-day helper cleanup. Only the name, preferences and coarse dates you explicitly save in your profile are shared with your trip. Booking evidence and references are not copied into the shared profile automatically.

Place searches and route origins and destinations are sent to Google Maps. Public research questions are sent to Gemini and public venue websites. These provider results are displayed transiently and are not stored in the shared book. Google's Privacy Policy is incorporated here by reference and describes Google's processing. Provider retention is governed by your operator's provider account and the applicable service terms; this app does not promise zero provider retention.

Discovery, dice-result and invitation maps load automatically as they approach the visible area. The place or host meeting-point search is sent to Google Maps. These maps do not request your device location. Opening directions lets you choose your starting point in Google Maps.

The discovery area map loads public OpenStreetMap tiles as it becomes visible. OpenStreetMap receives your IP address, browser referrer and the map tiles requested for the area you view. No profile, hotel, host meeting point or device location is sent to this tile service. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Website watches store your chosen public URL and limited readable text to detect changes. Watches and their updates are private to you. They do not email friends, continuously track your location or run past the selected deadline. Cancel them from My checks.

The trip owner can export a backup and administer access. Operational backups may retain previously saved content. Ask your trip owner about removal and backup retention before sharing sensitive information. Do not submit medical, payment or identity documents.
